## Who we are

The data controller is Atlas Media Network, LLC, 36 Russ Street, 3rd Floor, Hartford, CT 06106, United States. Privacy contact: [privacy@atlasmedianetwork.org](mailto:privacy@atlasmedianetwork.org).

## What we collect

The site has no forms, no accounts, no newsletter sign-up and no advertising or tracking cookies. Site search runs in your browser and we do not record what you search. If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply and to follow up on your tip or correction.

## Hosting and technical logs

The site is hosted by Netlify, which may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the site and keep it secure.

## Embedded video

Video stories may embed YouTube players in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). If you play a video, YouTube may process data under its own privacy policy.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you. Write to [privacy@atlasmedianetwork.org](mailto:privacy@atlasmedianetwork.org).
