## Ownership

Citizen Observer is a business unit of **Atlas Media Network, LLC**, 36 Russ Street, 3rd Floor, Hartford, CT 06106, United States. It is part of the Atlas Media Network family of outlets. [atlasmedianetwork.org](https://www.atlasmedianetwork.org)

Citizen Observer is not affiliated with any other publication, website or organization with a similar name.

## What we do

We report on politics, elections, policy, the economy and the states, and we check public claims against primary sources: statutes, court records, official data and on-the-record statements.

## Independence

Citizen Observer does not endorse parties, candidates or ballot measures in its news coverage. News and opinion are kept separate, and opinion is labeled as such.

## How we work

Every story carries a byline, a publication date and links to its sources. When something cannot be confirmed, we say so or leave it out. Our full [editorial standards](/standards/) explain how we report, how we use AI tools and how we correct mistakes.
