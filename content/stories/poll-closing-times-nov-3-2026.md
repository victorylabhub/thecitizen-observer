Polls close at different times across the country on Election Day, Tuesday, Nov. 3. Daylight saving time ends on Nov. 1, so the times below are **Eastern Standard Time**. Several states span two time zones, and the time listed is when most of the state's polls close.

| Most polls close (ET) | States |
|---|---|
| 6 p.m. | Indiana, Kentucky |
| 7 p.m. | Florida, Georgia, New Hampshire, South Carolina, Vermont, Virginia |
| 7:30 p.m. | North Carolina, Ohio, West Virginia |
| 8 p.m. | Alabama, Connecticut, Delaware, District of Columbia, Illinois, Kansas, Maine, Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan, Mississippi, Missouri, New Jersey, North Dakota, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas |
| 8:30 p.m. | Arkansas |
| 9 p.m. | Arizona, Colorado, Iowa, Louisiana, Minnesota, Nebraska, New Mexico, New York, Wisconsin, Wyoming |
| 10 p.m. | Idaho, Montana, Nevada, Utah |
| 11 p.m. | California, Oregon, Washington |
| Midnight | Alaska, Hawaii (early Nov. 4, Eastern) |

## States split by time zones

In **Indiana** and **Kentucky**, polls close at 6 p.m. local time, so counties on Central time close an hour later in Eastern terms, at 7 p.m. ET. **Florida**'s western Panhandle closes at 8 p.m. ET. Parts of **Texas, Michigan, Kansas, North Dakota and South Dakota** close at 9 p.m. ET, and Idaho's northern Panhandle at 11 p.m. ET. Alaska's westernmost Aleutian Islands close at 1 a.m. ET.

In **New Hampshire**, towns set their own closing times; most close at 7 p.m. and some larger cities at 8 p.m. **North Dakota** also allows local variation.

## Mail-voting states

Colorado, Hawaii, Oregon, Utah and Washington conduct elections mainly by mail. For those states, the time shown is the deadline to return a ballot in person.

## If you are in line

Anyone in line when polls close may still vote. Individual polling places can keep different hours, so confirm yours with your local election office through [vote.gov](https://vote.gov/).
